Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-31

What this app is

yt-tools is a private tool used only by its operator to manage the operator's own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public.

Data we access

When the operator signs in with Google, yt-tools receives OAuth tokens with these scopes, and uses them only for the purposes listed:

  • youtube.upload: upload videos and thumbnails to the operator's channels
  • youtube.force-ssl: edit titles, descriptions, tags and publish schedules of the operator's videos
  • youtube.readonly: read the operator's video list, titles and subscriber counts
  • yt-analytics.readonly: read statistics of the operator's channels (views, watch time, click-through rate)

How data is stored and shared

  • Tokens are stored only on the operator's own computer and are never published.
  • Channel statistics are kept only for the operator's own analysis.
  • We do not sell, rent or share Google user data with any third party, and do not use it for advertising.
  • yt-tools's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • yt-tools uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

How to revoke access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored tokens are deleted on request.
